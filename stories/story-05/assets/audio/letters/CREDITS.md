# English letter-name recording credits

Human US English pronunciation recordings hosted by Wikimedia Commons. These are letter names, not phonics sounds.

The WAV conversions retain each recording's license below. CC BY-SA applies to these recordings and their audio adaptations, not the independent story code or artwork. Public-domain dedications are preserved. No endorsement by the original speakers is implied.

Changes: Ogg originals or the official Wikimedia MP3 transcodes converted to mono 22050 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV; only outside silence trimmed with consonant margins; volume normalized; short leading/trailing silence added. Source files and checksums are preserved in `originals/` and `sources.json`. Rebuild offline with `python scripts/build_story05_letters.py`.

A uses /eɪ/; I uses /aɪ/; Z uses American /ziː/. The former Microsoft Zira spelling “ay” produced the same sound as “eye”; those TTS recordings are no longer used.
